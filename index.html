🔬 Dead Stock Risk Score (DSRS) Algorithm & Matrix
Mathematical formula and weighting model specified in PCE IT Department project slides
HEALTHY 0 — 30
Fast-moving stock with consistent outward transactions (< 45 days inactivity).
SLOW MOVING 31 — 60
Declining sales velocity (45–90 days inactivity). Requires promotional bundling.
HIGH RISK 61 — 80
Prolonged stagnation (90–180 days inactivity) with high carrying capital cost.
DEAD STOCK 81 — 100
Zero sales > 180 days. Immediate clearance discount or liquidation required.
DSRS Weighted Algorithmic Formula:
DSRS = (Inactivity_Score × 0.40) + (Stagnancy_Score × 0.30) + (Capital_Score × 0.20) + (Obsolescence_Score × 0.10)
• Inactivity (40%): Days since last sale vs 180-day threshold.
• Stagnancy (30%): Stock age vs standard product lifespan.
• Capital Lockup (20%): Total value tied up in stagnant inventory.
• Obsolescence (10%): Tech/electronics degradation risk factor.
